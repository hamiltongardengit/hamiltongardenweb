Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all services provided by Hamilton Garden Inn & Suites and its branches and affiliates (referred to collectively as "Hamilton Garden Inn & Suites" in the policy below). This policy outlines our procedures for handling information and complies with the Privacy Act. For any inquiries, please contact our Privacy team at info@hamiltongardeninnsuites.com.

Collection of Personally Identifiable Information

Hamilton Garden Inn & Suites collects personalized information solely for business purposes, adhering to the Privacy Act. We ensure that all information is collected in a fair and lawful manner. Personal information will only be used after authentication and permission from our members. This policy primarily addresses information exchange between Hamilton Garden Inn & Suites and its members and does not cover contractor and employee records, which are exempt under the Privacy Act.

Services

To enhance service standards, Hamilton Garden Inn & Suites collaborates with various service providers. These third parties may access member information to deliver services on our behalf. We strive to ensure that all service providers adhere to privacy guidelines and use information only for authorized purposes.

Usage of Information

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. Information is securely deleted or anonymized when it is no longer needed. We prioritize data security and use SSL encryption to protect information on our website. Cookies are employed to tailor service and support information to your computer and facilitate transactions. Cookies do not collect personal data but help us understand the type of computer being used. Accepting cookies improves your website experience without granting access to personal information. Hamilton Garden Inn & Suites values your privacy and ensures the protection of your personal information.

Security Of User Data:

Users have the right to access, correct, or delete their personal information. To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@hamiltongardeninnsuites.com.

Copyright and Security

Hamilton Garden Inn & Suites employs SSL encryption and other industry-standard security measures to protect user data. We implement strict access controls to ensure data is only accessible by authorized personnel.

Policy Updates

Hamilton Garden Inn & Suites may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on our website, and we encourage users to review the policy periodically.

Contact Information

For more details or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@hamiltongardeninnsuites.com or through our website's contact form.
